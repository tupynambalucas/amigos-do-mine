# Servers Using Magic

* **sandbox.elmakers.com** : [elMakers Sandbox](http://sandbox.elmakers.com) : The official Magic sandbox. Use the [corresponding website](http://sandbox.elmakers.com) to try out making your own spells!
* **mine.elmakers.com** : [elMakers Survival+](http://mine.elmakers.com) : The official Magic survival dev server. Come to preview the plugin, all builtin example configs are shown here, except for the RPG configs.
* **rpg.elmakers.com** : [elMakers RPG](http://mine.elmakers.com/?example=rpg) : The official Magic RPG dev server. Come to preview an example D&D-style RPG setup.

* **McPrime.space** : [PrimeSpace](https://mc-primespace.ru) : Russian Minecraft server, many interesting modes, including magic, with its own training and many interesting things! ([Русский](https://vk.com/prime_space))
* **play.potterworldmc.com** : [Potterworld](http://potterworldmc.com/) : A complete overhaul of Magic for a Harry Potter server. Wands, brooms, magic items, and more! This server has a custom texture pack with custom spell and wand icons.
* **play.rsmv.net** : [RSMV](http://www.rsmv.net) : Everything magic! Wands, Spells, Mobs, Recipes, Collectibles, Rituals! + more!!
* **play.peaceandcube.fr:10098** : [PeaceAndCube](https://peaceandcube.fr/) : A French survival server proposing custom wands from Magic via dungeons and quests.
* **play.badwolfmc.com** : [BadWolfMC Gamma](https://www.badwolfmc.com) : An 18+ server network with a Whovian twist: any sufficiently advanced technology is indistinguishable from magic! Our Gamma server features Magic with custom wands, disguises, brewing, dungeons, and quests!
* **magi-ka.fatemc.net** : [Magi-Ka] : A Magic Survival Factions Server with many features and Op Stats. Become a Op powerful Mage/Wizard and do friendly fights in the arena or take it to the real world and fight faction to faction but beware of loosing your stuff. We also host manhunts with or without magic, or other types of manhunt twists. Magic & Custom Spells, Epic World, Factions, Advanced Enchantments, Custom Ore Gen, Ore Crops, Player Market & Shops, Bosses & Leveled Mobs
